About Aafina

Turning ideas and intellectual property into scalable technology.

Aafina Technologies & Consulting Services Pvt. Ltd. is an innovation-driven technology and consulting company that turns ideas, intellectual property and emerging technologies into scalable products, platforms and business models.

We operate through two verticals. Aafina Technologies develops our own intellectual property and platforms, and partners with global technology providers for distribution. Aafina Consulting helps businesses re-engineer their business, marketing and distribution models for the post-AI economy.
